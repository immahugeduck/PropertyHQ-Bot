## Independent review and final verification

One independent read-only review across both repositories identified five product gaps. All five were reproduced before fixes and addressed in one fix pass:

1. Conditional entry: `You can enter if nobody is home, but only after 5 PM.` and `You have my permission to enter. But call me first.` no longer create unrestricted unattended-entry permission. The original restriction reaches maintenance; an old unrestricted proposal cannot be booked.
2. Mixed scheduling and permission: a rejection of offered times with an explicit entry refusal now saves availability, clears the pending choice and invalidates the old proposal. A subsequent `CONFIRM` cannot book the rejected slot. An explicit time rejection also clears the choice during an AI extraction outage, preserving the original text for staff.
3. Scheduled physical updates: a new grinding-noise report reaches maintenance even when no rescheduling is needed. Only an explicit entry clarification suppresses the maintenance alert.
4. Useful details alongside facts: an insecure main entry door and a newly supplied basement location stay in the work order even when structured facts accompany them.
5. Exact repeated BREE answer: a second `Yes permission granted.` with a different inbound SID reaffirms the same saved grant without another maintenance alert or proposal-version change. A passive answer cannot create fresh permission after a different kind of question.

Additional scope check: a mixed entry answer and distinct new repair cannot copy the prior job's permission onto the new repair.

Final local verification: **252 bot tests passed; 28 Property HQ tests passed; 61 focused review/workflow checks passed; bot syntax and both repository diff checks passed.** No product findings were deferred. The final fixes were checked with regression tests and the full suites; the independent reviewer was not asked for a second review.

Review scope decisions: live AI accuracy and carrier delivery remain outside these local fixtures, so live phrasing and delivery still require a staged check. Arbitrary free-text availability is retained for maintenance review rather than automatically converted into calendar constraints; maintenance must choose compatible times. Nothing in this work sends live messages, edits BREE's ticket, applies the migration in production or deploys either app.
