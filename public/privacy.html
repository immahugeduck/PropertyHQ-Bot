Privacy Policy

First Call Maintenance

Effective and last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how First Call Maintenance (“FCM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information associated with our maintenance request and text messaging services.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, mobile telephone number, service address or unit, maintenance request details, photographs you choose to provide, message content, scheduling information, and records of your consent, opt-out request, and communications with us.

How we use information

We use this information to receive and manage maintenance requests; provide troubleshooting or safety guidance; schedule service; send request confirmations, status updates, and completion notices; maintain service and consent records; protect the security of our systems; and comply with applicable law.

SMS consent and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with service providers is permitted only as necessary to support our messaging and maintenance services. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help deliver the messaging service or when required by law.

Participation in the SMS program is optional and is not a condition of receiving maintenance service. Message frequency varies based on your maintenance activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Service providers and disclosures

We may disclose information to vendors that process communications or host our application on our behalf, including telecommunications and hosting providers. These providers may use the information only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect safety or rights, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for maintenance operations, consent records, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and system security. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance. You may also contact First Call Maintenance through firstcallmaintenance.biz to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Children

Our maintenance messaging service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The effective date above will identify the latest version.
